Dataset overview

What the dataset contains

Download codebook →
01

1,985 German nouns

02

16 experiential dimensions

Size, numerosity, space, time, cognition, social interaction, arousal, emotion, valence, interoception, action, visual, auditory, tactile, olfactory, and gustatory.

03

Lexical ratings

Familiarity and concreteness with descriptive statistics.

04

Derived summaries

Participant counts, dominant experiential dimension, and the exclusivity score for each word.

05

Semantic annotations

Up to fourteen associated GermaNet semantic-field entries per lexical item.

Interactive visualization

Word profile visualization

Choose a word and switch between radar, bar, and heatmap views. The plots use the mean ratings across the 16 experiential dimensions.

All experiential dimensions are shown on the 1–7 rating scale; signed valence is rescaled to 1–7 for visualization. Use the camera icon in the Plotly toolbar to export the current figure as a PNG.

Interactive explorer

Search and compare words

Select a rating dimension, filter the records, and open any word for its complete experiential profile.

Reuse the resource

Download, cite, and reproduce

The downloadable files preserve German characters using UTF-8 encoding. The summary-level dataset contains item-level descriptive statistics and derived measures; the participant-level dataset provides the corresponding anonymized participant-level ratings.

Authors: Cristina Cara, Christian Bellebaum, Laura Bechtold, Marta Ghio

Affiliation: Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf, Faculty of Mathematics and Natural Sciences, Department of Biological Psychology, Germany

Correspondence: Cristina Cara, Department of Biological Psychology, Faculty of Mathematics and Natural Sciences, Heinrich Heine University Düsseldorf, Universitätsstraße 1, 40225 Düsseldorf, Germany . cristina.cara@hhu.de

Semantic annotations. Semantic-field annotations are included with permission from GermaNet. The resource is acknowledged and cited in the accompanying documentation and manuscript.

How to cite. Please cite the accompanying manuscript when using this resource. The preprint is available on PsyArXiv. After peer-reviewed publication, the final article will become the preferred citation.
